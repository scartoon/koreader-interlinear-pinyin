# Data files

`chars.tsv`, `words.tsv` and `defs.tsv` are generated by `tools/build_data.py`; `rules.tsv` and
`uncertain.tsv` by `tools/learn_from_cpp.py`. All five are licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They are derived from:

- CC-CEDICT, © MDBG and contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
- wordfreq data by Robyn Speer, CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
- pypinyin by mozillazg (character readings and its phrase list, `phrases_dict`), MIT License — https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin
- the CPP polyphone dataset by Kyubyong Park and Seanie Lee (train split only), https://github.com/kakaobrain/g2pM — repository Apache-2.0 with no separate licence for the sentences, which come from Chinese Wikipedia (CC BY-SA); the sentences themselves are not included

## How the files were made

Changes made: characters ranked by summed word frequency; default reading chosen by the
frequency of the words using each reading; dictionary limited to 2–4 character words
containing an annotatable polyphonic character; numbered pinyin converted to tone marks;
pypinyin phrases merged in (first reading of each syllable; where both sources have a word
and disagree, CC-CEDICT is kept unless it differs only by a neutral tone); neutral tones on
annotatable characters replaced by the character's toned reading with the same letters;
each word's frequency (per billion words, from wordfreq) added as a third column.

Hand-checked corrections in `tools/overrides.tsv` are applied last and win over both sources.

`rules.tsv`: char, L or R, the neighbouring character, the reading, and the number of training
examples (each rule: at least 5, at least 95% agreeing). `uncertain.tsv`: char, confidence in
its default (training data blended with the dictionary estimate), and its two most likely
readings, most likely first; characters below 75% confidence are listed.

`defs.tsv` (definitions for the tap popup; CC-CEDICT only): key, the word's frequency (0 for a
character), then pinyin and senses for each CC-CEDICT reading of the key. It covers every
annotatable character (rank above 1,000 or unranked) and every 2–4 character CC-CEDICT word
containing one, in simplified and traditional forms. Changes made: entries for the same key and
reading merged; numbered pinyin converted to tone marks; cross-references made readable
(`variant of 夠|够[gou4]` → `variant of 够 gòu`, `CL:個|个[ge4]` → `CL: 个 gè`, in the key's own
script); a sense that only points at the key itself (`椉 乘 /old variant of 乘[cheng2]/` under 乘)
dropped. Lines are sorted by the key's UTF-8 bytes so the plugin can binary-search the file.
